v1.0.0 - Unofficial campaign SPRITE crash repair

Fixes out-of-range monster species selection (12 candidates for 7 species) and
repairs invalid species in existing campaigns by assigning Minotauros.
Preserves campaign animations and the original campaign decision code.
Supports exact verified Steam build 8890160 and GOG 2.10E HD executables only.
Includes executable backup, status check, restore, and reinstall/reapply support.
Unknown versions are refused. See README.txt for installation and limitations.

Testing: 230 emulated machine-code cases per supported executable; installer
round-trip/reinstall/refusal checks; successful transition on one affected
campaign. Broader player testing is welcome.

The archive includes the readable PowerShell installer and patch data. It does
not include the complete game, personal saves, or memory dumps.
